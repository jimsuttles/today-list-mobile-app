# Privacy Policy — Today List

**Publisher:** 4CTech, LLC  
**Effective date:** August 28, 2026  
**Contact:** jim@4ctech.io

Today List is a local-first task app. Your task titles and notes are stored on your device. Today List does not require an account.

## What we store on your device

- Task titles, notes, list placement (Today / Later), reminders, repeat settings, and completion history
- App preferences (theme, unfinished-task behavior, week start, haptics, ads-removed cache)

This information stays on your device unless you choose to back up your device through your operating system or another tool you control.

## What we share with third parties

Today List uses Google services that may process limited device and usage data:

| Service | Purpose | Examples |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Google AdMob | Show banner ads for free users | Advertising ID, coarse device/app signals |
| Google Play Billing | One-time “Remove Ads” purchase and restore | Purchase tokens / Play account signals handled by Google Play |
| Firebase Analytics | Privacy-safe product metrics | App opens, feature usage counts — **not** task titles or notes |
| Firebase Crashlytics | Stability | Crash stacks and device metadata — **not** task titles or notes |

If you purchase Remove Ads, banner ads stop. Analytics and crash reporting may still run unless you disable them through your device settings where available.

## Data we do not collect as app content

We do not upload your task titles or notes to 4CTech servers. Today List has no 4CTech cloud sync in v1.0.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — only requested when you turn on a reminder, so we can alert you at the time you chose.
- **Alarms / exact alarms** (Android) — used to schedule reminders reliably when the system allows it.

## Retention and deletion

- Delete individual tasks or completions in the app.
- Clear History or Delete All App Data from Settings to remove local data on this device.
- Uninstalling the app removes local app data from that device (subject to OS backup behavior).

To request help with deletion or privacy questions, email jim@4ctech.io.

## Children

Today List is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
